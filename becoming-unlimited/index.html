Free on Zoom Tuesdays · 7:00–8:30 pm Toronto (EST) Join from anywhere

Becoming Unlimited is Ajita's free weekly healing and awakening circle — a place to shift your energy, dissolve emotional wounds, and return to your power. You can keep your camera off and say nothing at all. Many people sit quietly for months before they ever speak.

Each week, Ajita guides you through

  • Short but powerful teachings on healing energy and consciousness
  • Practical tools for real transformation in health, love, money and relationships
  • A Q&A space to receive guidance or clarity on your path

What the circle explores

Ancient wisdom and strategies, karmic healing, identity shifting, breathwork, meditations, shadow work, inner-child work, self-love and confidence building — different doorways each week, all in support of your personal evolution.

How would it be if you could…

  • Move out of your current situation and step into the version of you who already is, has, and does everything you desire
  • Confidently step into your authentic self — unapologetically seen and heard
  • Bring your vision into tangible reality through aligned action, powerful healing and mindset tools
  • Develop unshakable confidence in your abilities and clarity about your goals — no longer held back by self-doubt
  • Get clear on the real steps that create real fulfillment, personally and professionally

More than a weekly call

A community of like-minded souls. A private, safe space to share and connect with others on the same personal and professional journey as you.

Priority access to Ajita's offerings. Early access to her programs and events, community-only discounts, and first invitation to retreats.

The circle is spiritual and energy work, not medical or psychological care, and it is not a substitute for professional help. Come as you are — there is nothing to prepare.
