How to Gauge Energy from People

April 11, 2023 · 5 min read · Ajita Shah

Energy is a fundamental aspect of human existence that is reflected in your emotions, thoughts, and actions. In human beings, energy is manifested in different ways, ranging from positive and uplifting to negative and draining.

The energy you emit has a profound impact on those around you, as well as your own experiences. Your energy attracts or repels people and opportunities, influencing the trajectory of your life. For example, individuals who emit positive energy tend to attract positive people and experiences into their lives, while those who emit negative energy struggle to form meaningful connections with others and face difficulties in achieving their goals.

What is Positive Energy?

Positive energy is characterized by feelings of love, joy, peace, and compassion. People who radiate positive energy are often described as being warm, inviting, and uplifting. They bring a sense of calm and stability to those around them and create a positive environment wherever they go. This type of energy is often contagious and has a profound impact on those around them, leading to increased happiness and well-being.

Cultivating positive energy is done through practices such as meditation, mindfulness, and self-reflection. By focusing on these practices, individuals gain insight into their thoughts and emotions and work to change negative patterns. Additionally, engaging in activities that bring joy and fulfilment, also helps increase positive energy and reduce negative energy.

What is Negative Energy?

Negative energy is characterized by feelings of anger, fear, and frustration. People who emit negative energy often leave those around them feeling drained and heavy. Negative energy also blocks personal growth and limits the ability to achieve goals. This type of energy creates a toxic environment and negatively impacts relationships with others.

How to Sense and Gauge Positive and Negative Energy

Sensing and gauging positive and negative energy in others is an important skill that helps you better understand and interact with those around you. Here are a few tips for recognizing and interpreting energy:

  • Observe body language and facial expressions: Your bodies often reflect your inner feelings and emotions. Pay attention to posture, facial expressions, and gestures, as these provide clues about a person’s energy. For example, someone who is relaxed and confident has an open posture, while someone who is nervous has a closed posture or crossed arms.
  • Listen to tone of voice: The tone of a person’s voice also reveals their energy. A person who is happy and positive has a warm and upbeat tone, while someone who is negative has a harsh or monotone voice.
  • Pay attention to emotions: Your emotions are closely tied to your energy. When interacting with others, pay attention to the emotions they express, both verbally and non-verbally. This helps you gauge the energy they are radiating.
  • Trust your intuition: Sometimes, your intuition also provides you with important information about a person’s energy. If you have a strong gut feeling about someone’s energy, it’s worth paying attention to.
  • Practice empathy: Empathy involves putting yourself in another person’s shoes and trying to understand their perspective. When you can empathize with someone, you are able to better understand their energy and what is driving their emotions.

It’s important to remember that energy changes over time and in different situations, so it’s always a good idea to pay attention to patterns and trends in a person’s energy, rather than focusing on isolated incidents. By becoming more aware of the energy that others emit, you will be able to improve your relationships, avoid toxic people, and create a more positive and fulfilling life for yourself.

How to Protect Yourself from Negative Energy

By following the above tips, you will be able to sense and gauge negative energy that is felt and is often associated with negative emotions, thoughts, and experiences. Negative energy comes from many sources, including people, circumstances, and places. It causes feelings of anger, stress, anxiety, and sadness, and has a significant impact on your mental and emotional well-being.

Negative energy is infectious and spreads from person to person. It also builds up over time, causing you to feel drained and stressed. Negative energy tends to impact your daily life by making you feel more irritable, less focused, and less productive.

Negative energy has a significant impact on your life, draining your happiness. Here are a few strategies for protecting yourself from negative energy:

  • Surround yourself with positive people: Spending time with people who radiate positive energy helps counteract negative energy and boost your mood. Seek out relationships with individuals who are supportive, uplifting, and bring joy to your life.
  • Practice self-care: Engage in activities that bring you joy and restore your energy, such as exercise, meditation, or reading. Taking care of yourself helps build your resilience against negative energy and reduce its impact.
  • Set boundaries: It’s important to be aware of the energy you allow into your life and to set boundaries around toxic individuals and situations. This means limiting time spent with negative people, avoiding situations that trigger stress or anxiety, or setting clear limits around what you will and won’t tolerate.
  • Use visualization techniques: Visualization techniques help protect you from negative energy by imagining a positive and protective barrier around yourself. You will visualize a white light surrounding you and shielding you from negative energy.
  • Ground yourself: Negative energy leaves you feeling ungrounded and disoriented. Practicing grounding techniques, such as meditating, connecting with nature, or doing yoga, helps you reconnect with the present moment and restore a sense of balance.
  • Practice gratitude: Focusing on the positive aspects of your life helps counteract negative energy and promote happiness and well-being. Try to regularly focus on the things you are grateful for and express gratitude to those in your life who bring joy and positivity.
  • Seeking help: Seeking help from a professional therapist or counsellor is beneficial in managing negative energy.

By implementing these strategies, you will protect yourself from negative energy. It’s important to remember that these practices take time and effort, but with practice, you will develop a strong resilience against negative energy and create more positivity around yourself.
